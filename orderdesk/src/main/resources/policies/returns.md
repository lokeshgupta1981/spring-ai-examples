# Returns and Refunds

## Return window

You can return most items within 10 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original box with all tags.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Upload a photo of the item and the parcel on the order page. We send a replacement or give a full refund, and you do not pay for the return pickup.

## Items you cannot return

- Earphones and headphones once the seal is opened
- Groceries and other perishable items
- Gift cards

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the item. If you choose store credit instead, it is added to your OrderDesk wallet at once.
