# Payments

## Payment methods

We accept UPI, debit cards, credit cards and net banking.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to INR 5,000. It costs INR 30 extra per order.

## EMI

No-cost EMI is available on credit cards for orders above INR 3,000, for 3 or 6 months.

## Failed payments

If money is taken from your account but the order is not placed, it is refunded automatically within 3 working days.
