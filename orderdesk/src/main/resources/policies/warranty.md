# Warranty

## Standard warranty

Electronics sold on OrderDesk come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. Keep the invoice from the order page; the service centre asks for it.

## OrderDesk Care

OrderDesk Care adds one more year of warranty for INR 299. You can buy it only within 30 days of delivery.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover water damage, a cracked screen, or damage from a fall. It also does not cover items repaired by an unauthorised shop.

## How to claim

Open the order page, choose the item and click Claim Warranty. A pickup is arranged within 3 working days.
