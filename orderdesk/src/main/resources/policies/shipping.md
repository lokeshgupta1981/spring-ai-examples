# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we deliver

We deliver to every city in India. We do not deliver outside India.

## Delivery time

Orders to Pune, Mumbai and Delhi arrive in 2 days. Orders to every other city in India arrive in 5 days. Orders placed after 2 PM are shipped the next working day.

## Delivery charges

Delivery is free for orders above INR 499. Orders of INR 499 or less pay a delivery charge of INR 49.

## Express delivery

Express delivery is available in Pune, Mumbai and Delhi for INR 99. An express order placed before 2 PM arrives the next day.
